# Tantal

Your Android. With your AI.

Tantal is Android firmware for Pixel devices: one design across the whole
system, without Google.

- **Everything included.** Phone, Messages, Gallery, Calendar, Clock,
  Calculator, Music, Weather, Recorder, Keyboard and Notepad, built as one
  Material You suite with colors from your wallpaper and no Google services.
- **A launcher of your own.** Gestures, hidden apps and deep settings for
  the grid, icons and folders.
- **On-device AI.** A system service in Rust runs models on the phone's
  Tensor chip: speech recognition, translation, text to speech. It has no
  network access, and apps reach it only with your permission.

Tantal is based on GrapheneOS and keeps its protection; every security
difference is listed on [the site](https://tantal-os.github.io/docs/vs-grapheneos).
It is not affiliated with the GrapheneOS project.

Tantal is in active development and cannot be installed yet.
